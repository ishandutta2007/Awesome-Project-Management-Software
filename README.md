# Awesome-Project-Management-Software

## Top Project Management Software Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Task Planning, Team Collaboration & Project Delivery*

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial project management platforms** and **open-source projects** that help teams plan work, assign tasks, track progress, and deliver projects on time. These tools range from simple kanban boards to enterprise portfolio management systems.



**Examples** include Microsoft Project, Asana, Monday.com, ClickUp, Jira, Wrike, Smartsheet, Basecamp, Teamwork, and Workfront (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Project management is one of the strongest open-source domains. **OpenProject** leads as the most comprehensive open-source solution with Gantt, Agile, and portfolio management . **Plane** brings a modern, AI-native alternative to Jira and ClickUp . **Focalboard**, **Vikunja**, and **Wekan** provide lightweight kanban boards, while **Leantime** focuses on accessibility and non-project managers. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Microsoft Project](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/project/project-management-software)**

  **The enterprise standard for project portfolio management** — Gantt charts, resource management, and Microsoft 365 integration. **Subscription-based** from $10/user/month. **The reference for traditional project management**.



- **[Asana](https://asana.com/)**

  **The most user-friendly project management platform** — list, board, timeline, and calendar views with strong collaboration features. **Free for up to 15 users**; paid from $10.99/user/month. **The best choice for marketing and operations teams**.



- **[Monday.com](https://monday.com/)**

  **The most customizable Work OS** — flexible boards, automations, and dashboards. **Free for up to 2 seats**; paid from $9/seat/month. **The best for visual workflow management**.



- **[ClickUp](https://clickup.com/)**

  **The most feature-complete all-in-one platform** — tasks, docs, goals, chat, and whiteboards. **Generous free tier**; paid from $7/user/month. **The best value for teams wanting everything in one tool**.



- **[Jira](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira)**

  **The standard for software development teams** — Agile boards, backlog management, and deep developer tool integration. **Free for up to 10 users**; paid from $7.53/user/month. **The reference for Agile project management**.



- **[Wrike](https://www.wrike.com/)**

  **The best for enterprise project portfolio management** — resource management, proofing, and automation. **Free tier available**; paid from $9.80/user/month. **The best for marketing and professional services**.



- **[Smartsheet](https://www.smartsheet.com/)**

  **The spreadsheet-based project management platform** — Gantt charts, automations, and enterprise governance. **Paid only** from $9/user/month. **The best for PMO and operations teams**.



- **[Basecamp](https://basecamp.com/)**

  **The simplest project management tool** — message boards, to-dos, schedules, and files. **Flat pricing** ($299/month for unlimited users). **The best for small teams wanting simplicity**.



- **[Teamwork](https://www.teamwork.com/)**

  **The best for client-facing project management** — time tracking, billing, and client portals. **Free tier available**; paid from $5.99/user/month. **The best for agencies and consultancies**.



- **[Workfront](https://www.workfront.com/)**

  **Adobe's enterprise work management platform** — portfolio management, resource planning, and enterprise-grade governance. **Enterprise pricing only**. **The best for large organizations with complex workflows**.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject)**

  **The leading open-source project management suite**, AGPLv3 licensed with **14,700+ GitHub stars** . **Full-featured with tasks (Work Packages), Gantt charts, Agile boards (Scrum/Kanban), meetings, time tracking, cost management, and documentation** . Recent 17.6/17.9 releases add **XWiki integration**, **sprint goals**, **backlog buckets**, **task creation from documents**, and **parallel Jira migration** with resumable imports . Docker deployment recommended; enterprise edition adds support and advanced features . **The de facto open-source Microsoft Project alternative** — the most complete open-source PM platform .



- **[Plane](https://github.com/makeplane/plane)**

  **Modern open-source alternative to Jira, Linear, Monday, and ClickUp**, AGPLv3 licensed with **47,800+ GitHub stars** . **AI-native project management** with Work Items, Cycles (sprints with burn-down charts), Modules (grouping related work), Pages (wiki with AI), and five view layouts (List, Board, Spreadsheet, Gantt, Calendar) . Integrations with GitHub, GitLab, Slack, Sentry; importers from Jira, Linear, Asana, ClickUp, Monday . Deployable via Docker AIO container on Railway or self-hosted . **The most modern open-source PM platform** — best for software teams wanting a Jira alternative .



- **[Focalboard](https://github.com/mattermost-community/focalboard)**

  **Open-source, self-hosted alternative to Trello, Notion, and Asana**, MIT/AGPL/Apache licensed . **Kanban boards, table views, and gallery views** for individuals and teams . Maintained by Mattermost community . **Best for lightweight kanban project management** .



- **[Wekan](https://github.com/wekan/wekan)**

  **Open-source Trello-like kanban board**, MIT licensed . **Real-time collaboration, swimlanes, and card dependencies** . **Best for simple kanban boards** .



- **[Leantime](https://github.com/Leantime/leantime)**

  **PHP-based project management designed for non-project managers**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **10,200+ GitHub stars** . **Accessibility features for ADHD, autism, and dyslexia** . Kanban, Gantt, calendar, and table views with goal tracking, wikis, and Slack/Mattermost integrations . LDAP/OIDC authentication, S3 storage, REST API, 20+ languages . **Best for teams wanting simplicity and accessibility** .



- **[Vikunja](https://github.com/go-vikunja/vikunja)**

  **Open-source task management with hierarchical structure**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **Smart recurring tasks and Telegram integration** . List/table/gantt views . **Best for personal and small team task management** .



- **[Kanboard](https://github.com/kanboard/kanboard)**

  **Simple visual task board**, MIT licensed . **Minimalist kanban with drag-and-drop, swimlanes, and plugin ecosystem** . **Best for teams wanting the simplest possible kanban** .



- **[4ga Boards](https://github.com/RARgames/4gaBoards)**

  **Straightforward real-time kanban boards**, MIT licensed . **Elegant dark mode, collapsible todo lists, and multitasking tools** . Node.js/Docker/K8S deployment . **Best for modern, lightweight kanban** .



- **[Orangescrum Community Edition](https://github.com/Orangescrum/opensource-community-edition)**

  **Free self-hosted project management with no seat limit**, GNU AGPL v3 . Released August 2026 with CakePHP 4, PHP 8.2+, PostgreSQL 16 . Projects, tasks, subtasks, list/Kanban/calendar/overview views, custom task statuses, checklists, milestones, time logging, comments, mentions, labels, saved search filters, task reminders, roles/permissions, personal dashboards, file attachments, 2FA, REST API . **Not included**: Scrum/sprints, Gantt charts, resource management, timesheets, budget/cost, invoicing, defect tracking, test cases, document management, wiki, risk management, AI chat, MCP server, SSO . **Best for self-hosted teams wanting core PM without proprietary licensing** .



- **[Paca](https://github.com/Paca-AI/paca)**

  **AI-native project management platform with MCP server**, MIT licensed . **Connects AI agents directly to workspace data** — projects, tasks, sprints, documents, members, roles, task types/statuses, views, custom fields, attachments, activity/comments . **Claude Code skills for managing workspace via natural-language slash commands** . Docker deployment with PostgreSQL, Valkey . **Best for AI-agent-driven project management** .



- **[WorkBase](https://github.com/vocso-com/WorkBase)**

  **Local-first desktop project manager (offline Trello alternative)**, open-source . **Unlimited nesting (project → module → task → subtask), weighted progress rollup, four views (Board, Kanban, Outline, Projects home)** . My Work across all projects, 15 templates, dependencies with blocked-state propagation, full-text search, export to PNG/PDF/Markdown/CSV/JSON . Tauri-based (Rust + Node) for macOS and Windows; local-only storage, no accounts . **Best for offline, private project management** .



- **[Redmine](https://github.com/redmine/redmine)**

  **Classic open-source issue tracking and project management**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Gantt charts, calendars, wikis, forums, and role-based access** . Mature ecosystem with hundreds of plugins . **Best for organizations wanting a veteran open-source PM tool** .



- **[Taiga](https://github.com/kaleidos-ventures/taiga)**

  **Agile project management for Scrum and Kanban teams**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **Backlog management, sprint planning, and kanban boards** . **Best for Agile teams wanting open-source Scrum** .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **epicd** — Markdown-native task manager and kanban visualizer for any Git repository, MIT licensed, AI-ready with MCP support for Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Codex .

- **Jotter** — Local-first privacy-focused task management stored as Markdown files, Kanban/list/Eisenhower Matrix views, git sync, Android app, MCP support .

- **AppFlowy** — Open-source Notion alternative with to-do lists, kanban, and databases, AGPL-3.0 licensed .

- **Donetick** — Task and chore management for personal/family use with scheduling, assignment, and group sharing, Go-based .

- **Nullboard** — Single-page minimalist kanban board, BSD-2-Clause licensed, compact and highly readable .

- **Taskwarrior** — Command-line TODO list manager, flexible, fast, and unobtrusive .

- **Mimrai** — Lightweight open-source task management (early stage), AGPL-3.0 for non-commercial use .

- **Projectlibre** — Open-source Microsoft Project alternative with Gantt charts, network diagrams, and resource management .

- **Redmine** — Classic open-source issue tracking and project management .



**Frameworks for building custom project management solutions**: Combine **OpenProject** for full-featured project management with Gantt, Agile, and wiki integration . Use **Plane** for modern AI-native issue tracking with cycles and modules . Deploy **Focalboard** or **Wekan** for lightweight kanban . Choose **Leantime** for accessibility-focused teams or those seeking Trello simplicity with Jira features . Use **Orangescrum Community Edition** for self-hosted teams wanting core PM without proprietary licensing . Integrate **Paca** for AI-agent-driven project management with MCP . Note that true enterprise project portfolio management with resource management, portfolio planning, and compliance certifications remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong task, project, and collaboration foundations that require integration for complete enterprise deployment.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Project management tools handle sensitive project and organizational data. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and backup procedures.

- **Open-source PM platforms vary significantly in maturity** — OpenProject and Plane are production-ready; Orangescrum Community Edition explicitly excludes Scrum, Gantt, timesheets, budget, and document management . Evaluate gaps before deployment.

- **Redmine and Taiga have different strengths** — Redmine for classic issue tracking with wikis; Taiga for Agile Scrum/Kanban . Choose based on methodology.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong task, project, and collaboration foundations, but **enterprise portfolio management, resource management, and compliance certifications** remain primarily commercial offerings.
